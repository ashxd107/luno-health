Type

Use it well

Do
  • Use the colour logo on white or Pearl, the white logo on Night or Deep Green.
  • Use one-colour versions for stamps, embroidery, engraving and fax.
  • Keep clear space around the logo at least the width of the crescent.
Don't
  • Stretch, rotate, recolour or add shadows.
  • Put the colour logo on busy photos; use the white version on a dark overlay.
  • Recreate the wordmark in another font.
