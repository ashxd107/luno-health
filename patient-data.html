When you send reports

You ask us to share them with hospitals you choose, so they can give an opinion and a price. We remove your name where we can before a first review.

Family

If a relative writes for you, we treat their number as the contact until you say otherwise.

Stop or delete

Write to care@lunohealth.com. We stop sharing and delete what we hold, except what the law or a hospital file must keep.
