About

India’s best hospitals, made simple for the world.

We started Luno Health because world-class treatment in India should be easy to reach for any family, from any country.

Our story

Same medicine. Fair price. Real care.

India’s leading hospitals treat patients from across the world with the same accreditation, technology and specialist expertise found in the best centres elsewhere, at a fraction of the cost. What families struggle with is everything around it: which hospital, which doctor, what it really costs, the visa, the language, the stay.

That is what we do. We bring your case to the right specialists, get you a written plan and price, and look after you and your family until you are home, with the warmth India is known for. Atithi Devo Bhava: the guest is God.

We are a facilitator, not a hospital. We do not give medical advice. Doctors at accredited partner hospitals decide your treatment. You pay the hospital directly; hospitals pay us, so our help is free for you.
