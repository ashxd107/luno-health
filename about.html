About

A quiet desk. Hospitals in India.

Luno Health is a facilitator. Not a hospital. We take patients to NABH hospitals in India — especially families who already fly the Delhi route and want the file handled in Russian.

We do not give medical advice. Doctors at the hospital decide treatment. You pay the hospital. Hospitals pay us.

We speak Russian, the language you speak at home, and English.
