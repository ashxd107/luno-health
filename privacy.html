Privacy policy

How we handle information when we arrange treatment in India.

Last updated October 2026 · Luno Health Pvt. Ltd., India

Who we are

Luno Health is a medical travel facilitator. We are not a hospital. We do not give medical advice. Partner hospitals are NABH-accredited. Labs are NABL-accredited.

What we collect

Name, phone, country, city, email, and what you write on the form or WhatsApp. If you send reports, we use them only to get a hospital opinion and quote.

Who we share with

Hospitals you agree to contact. Visa and travel partners when needed. WhatsApp / Meta when you message us. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

Write to lunohealthpe@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your file.
