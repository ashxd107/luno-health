Questions

What patients ask us first.

Is the quality really the same?

We work with NABH-accredited hospitals, many of which also hold JCI accreditation, an international standard used to assess hospitals worldwide. Lower prices in India come from lower operating costs, not lower standards.

Do I pay Luno Health anything?

No. You pay the hospital directly at its published international price. Hospitals pay us for coordinating your care.

Which languages do you speak?

Russian and English on every case, and we arrange interpreters for your home language, on WhatsApp and at the hospital.

How soon can I travel?

Written opinion in 48 hours. An Indian medical e-Visa usually takes 3–5 working days. Many surgeries can be scheduled within 2–3 weeks.

Can my family come with me?

Yes. The hospital invitation can include up to two companions, and we arrange accommodation for the whole family.
